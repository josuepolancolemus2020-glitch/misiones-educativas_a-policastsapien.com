Resultados de aprendizaje y Criterios de evaluación Resultados de aprendizaje Criterios de evaluación RA1. To express fluently orally and written, habits and routines in the specific field using the simple present, continuous and present perfect tense. CE1.1. To name and identify technical vocabulary used for commercial documents. CE1.2 To apply reading comprehension strategies, to identify characteristics of commercial documents (Memorandum, business letter, Bill of exchange, promissory note, checks, receipt, employment application, resume) and discuss their elements. CE1.3. To produce written texts about the difference between the varieties of commercial documents. CE1.4 To compose an example of each commercial document. RA2. To describe previous activities or experiences lived in the accounting laboratory, using oral and written language, applying the regular and irregular verbs in past tense. CE2.1 To identify the written form of regular and irregular verbs in a set of written texts and/or during didactic games. CE2.2. To recognize and produce the ending sounds of regular verbs in past tense (–ed. /id/ e.g. started, /t/ e.g. cooked, and /d/ e.g. rained) through listening and reading exercises. CE2.3. To develop a short presentation, using visual aids, describing an experience lived (personal or in the accounting lab), using technical vocabulary and previous grammar. CE2.4. To apply the correct structures and vocabulary during a role-play about a business fair (or any other topic) using past and present continuous tense. RA3. To use be going to and will to talk about plans and to describe how to use a specific equipment to perform a specific work assignment. CE3.1. To design a life plan using the future grammar structure, in order to give a short oral presentation to the class. CE3.2. To associate the proper structure of future tense through organizing sentences exercises, using technical language.

CE3.3. To list and describe the correct use of the working equipment used in a professional context, using be going to and will. RA4. To use technical vocabulary, terms and grammatical structures of modals verbs (must / should / have-has / might-may) to represent situations in a professional context. CE4.1. To write a manual (instructions) about accounting procedures. Examples: Posting the transactions into journal books, Determining the trial balance, others. CE4.2. To perform specific accounting related procedures, by listening and following instructions correctly. CE4.3. To describe the hygiene and safety rules that must be considered in a professional environment. CE4.4. To write and present a dialogue between two employees to develop efficiently in a working environment. RA5. To differentiate the usage of the auxiliary Can to express abilities and ask for permission. CE.5.1 To identify the purpose (abilities and permissions) of Can in texts and dialogues. CE.5.2. To use Can during role-plays asking for permissions and using vocabulary and procedures related to their field. CE.5.3. To use Can in written texts to express abilities using vocabulary and procedures related to business administration. RA6. To identify the acronyms related to the specific field and apply them correctly according to the activity. CE6.1. To list the most used in Business Administration acronyms and write their meanings. CE6.2. To implement the acronyms correctly in reading comprehension and written activities.
